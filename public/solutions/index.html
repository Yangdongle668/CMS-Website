Custom-program FAQ

Common questions before kick-off.

What's the minimum order quantity for a fully custom design?

3,000 pcs is the typical custom MOQ once we ramp. For prototypes during the program (A/B/C samples) we accept 100–500 pcs.

Is there an NRE for tooling and design work?

Tooling NRE depends on the geometry; a typical pouch-cell tool is USD 8–15k. Design NRE is usually waived if you commit to a forecast above 50k pcs/year. We'll be transparent in the feasibility quote.

Can you sign a quality agreement?

Yes — standard for medical and tier-1 customers. We use either your template or our IATF-aligned template, signed before MP starts.

Do you support second-source qualification?

Yes. If we're your second source we will match drawings exactly and run a 4-week qualification including dimensional, capacity, IR, abuse and cycle. We don't ask to redesign.

What if my volumes are smaller than 3,000 pcs?

Below MOQ we can keep the program on the pilot line with a per-piece adder. We've run continuous 500-pcs/month medical programs that way for years.
