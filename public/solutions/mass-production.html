Operations

How a typical MP program runs.

Pre-production

Process FMEA, control plan and first-article inspection. Typical duration: 3–4 weeks.

Ramp-up

First lot at a conservative fraction of forecasted volume. SPC monitoring for capacity, IR and thickness. Ramp to full volume over 2–3 lots.

Steady state

Monthly review with cpk, ppm defect, on-time delivery and CAPA status. Forecasts locked typically 8 weeks out.

End of life

We commit to last-time-buy notice ahead of any discontinuation — a hard ask in this industry, but one of the reasons our customers stay.
