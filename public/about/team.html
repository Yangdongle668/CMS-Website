Team

A hundred engineers, operators and specialists — one building, one shared line.

Overview

From fifteen to a hundred.

Zufek started in 2018 with a small founding team of roughly fifteen people with backgrounds in consumer-battery R&D and production. Eight years in, we’re a hundred strong — still the same tight team that can put a dedicated project engineer on every customer program from kick-off through mass production.

How we’re organised

Four groups, one line.

R

R&D

Materials, cell design, BMS firmware, test and analysis. Supports both new-chemistry qualification and customer-specific program work.

E

Engineering

Project engineers assigned 1-to-1 with major customer programs. Single point of contact from RFQ through PMS.

Q

Quality

Incoming inspection, in-process quality, outgoing release, and CAPA. Runs the ISO 9001 system end to end.

M

Manufacturing

The operators who actually build the cells — the biggest single group at Zufek.

S

Supply & Logistics

Materials planning, DGR-compliant packaging and international shipping. Responsible for every UN 38.3 / IEC 62133 document that leaves the building.

B

Commercial

Sales, customer service and post-shipment support for Greater China and overseas markets.

Working with us

One engineer, whole program.

Every customer gets a named project engineer on day one. That engineer stays with the program through sampling, qualification, mass production and after-sales. No handoffs between unknown teams, no ticket queues — just one person who knows your device and can escalate inside Zufek when needed.
