Company Profile

Who we are, what we do, and how we got here.

Mission

Fit the battery to the product, not the other way.

We exist because too many devices get quietly de-scoped around the battery — the thickness creeps up, runtime drops, a feature gets cut. Our job is to push that compromise back to the physics: the smallest, lightest, safest cell the chemistry will allow.

Overview

A focused battery house in Dongguan.

Founded 2018

Zufek was founded in 2018 in Dongguan with a small core team of engineers from the consumer-battery industry. The goal from day one was to be a specialist in custom small-format lithium cells for wearables, AR, medical and IoT — not another general-purpose cell factory.

100+ people today

From a starting team of around fifteen, Zufek has grown to a hundred engineers, operators and commercial staff. The majority of the team sits in R&D and production; the rest supports sales, quality, and global logistics from our Dongguan office.

Tier-1 OEM track record

We have delivered custom cell and pack programs into top-tier global OEMs across the smartphone, AR and semiconductor ecosystems. Program details are held under NDA; references available on request after initial NDA exchange.

Quality System

ISO 9001, running day-to-day.

Zufek operates an ISO 9001 quality-management system. Every program is assigned a dedicated project engineer, with documented design-review gates, change control and full lot traceability from incoming material through shipped cell.

Ownership

Privately held.

Zufek is privately held by its founders and senior engineers. No outside board seats, no quarterly earnings pressure — which is one of the reasons we can take on multi-year programs without blinking.

Focus

Three bets for the next three years.

Thinner pouches for next-gen AR — ongoing investment in sub-millimetre cell geometry.
Higher-voltage platforms — 4.45 V and 4.48 V LCO for density-sensitive wearables.
Deeper co-engineering — earlier involvement in customer ID to move beyond a commodity-supplier relationship.
