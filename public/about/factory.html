Dongguan — HQ, R&D & Production

R&D and production under one roof.

Our Dongguan facility houses the materials lab, cell design group, pilot line, production and QA — all in one place. This means program kick-off, sampling, and mass production share the same engineers and the same equipment settings, which keeps process knowledge from getting lost between buildings.

Materials & testing

In-house test chambers for temperature, humidity, vibration, shock, drop, short circuit and overcharge. All the test conditions required to pre-screen against UN 38.3 and IEC 62133 before samples leave our door.

Pilot line

Coating, calendering, slitting, winding, stacking, tab-welding, packaging and formation — the full cell-making sequence sized for A/B/C-sample runs of 100 to 5,000 pcs.

Production line

Automated assembly for mid-volume custom programs. Every cell is 100%-screened for capacity, internal resistance and self-discharge, and carries a laser-engraved serial linking back to its process data.
