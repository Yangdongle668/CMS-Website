Production Lines

Five lines, each tuned for its product.

All five run on the same quality system but use equipment optimised for the geometry. Process parameters from the pilot line transfer cleanly into MP.

L1

Polymer Li-Po (MP)

Z-stacking up to 60 layers, vacuum sealing, formation in 8 stages. Capacity 600k cells / mo. Capable of 0.4 mm minimum thickness, 200 × 150 mm maximum footprint.

L2

Custom-Shape Li-Po (MP)

In-house tooling shop — new geometry tools delivered in 25-35 days. Round, curved (R ≥ 25 mm), stepped, notched. Capacity 250k cells / mo.

L3

Cylindrical Li-Ion (MP)

14500, 18650, 21700, 26650, 32700 formats. Korean and Japanese automation. Laser-welded current collectors, CID safety vents, PTC. Capacity 650k cells / mo.

L4

Pack Assembly

Spot and laser welding, BMS integration, plastic and aluminium housings. IP65 / IP67 ratings. CAN, RS485, SMBus protocols. Capacity 50k packs / mo.

L5

Cleanroom (Medical)

Class 100K cleanroom for IEC 60601-aligned production. Gowned operators, particle counters, DHF documentation. Single-use sterile-pack assembly available.

PL

Pilot Line (NPI)

Identical equipment to L1/L2 at 1/10 scale. Where new geometries get qualified before MP transfer. Process parameters log directly into the MP MES.

Quality Control

Inspection at every stage.

100% in-line inspection on every cell — no statistical sampling shortcuts. Reports flow into your customer-specific dashboard within 24 hours of production.

01

Incoming materials

Cathode, anode, separator, electrolyte, casing — QC sampled from every lot. Annual supplier audits.

  • ICP-MS purity check
  • Dimensional CMM
  • Supplier CoA verified
02

In-process

Coating thickness, alignment, slitting and stacking. SPC charts on every line, every shift.

  • Beta-gauge thickness
  • CCD edge alignment
  • Sealing peel-strength
03

Formation & grading

Multi-step formation curves, OCV/IR/capacity grading. A/B grade separation per spec.

  • 8-step formation
  • 3-point grading (V, IR, capacity)
  • Self-discharge after 7-day rest
04

Final shipment

AQL sampling on packaging, drop test on cartons, full UN 38.3 documentation per lot.

  • OCV stability after 7-day rest
  • Carton drop test
  • UN 38.3 + MSDS attached
