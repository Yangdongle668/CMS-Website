Production Lines

Five purpose-built lines under one quality system.

Each line uses equipment optimised for its geometry, but they share document control, supplier audits, formation parameters and the same MES. Process changes discovered on the pilot line transfer to MP cleanly within hours.

L1

Polymer Li-Po (MP)

Z-stacking up to 60 layers, vacuum sealing, formation in 8 stages. Capable of 0.4 mm minimum thickness, 200 × 150 mm maximum footprint. Capacity ~50k cells / month.

L2

Custom-Shape Li-Po (MP)

In-house tooling shop — new geometry tools delivered in 25-35 days. Round, curved (R ≥ 25 mm), stepped, notched. Capacity ~25k cells / month.

L3

Coin Steel-Shell Lithium (MP)

LIR1620 / LIR2025 / LIR2032 / LIR2450 / LIR2477 (Li-ion 3.6 V) and ML2032 / ML2430 (Li-MnO₂ 3.0 V) formats. Hermetic crimp, reflow-compatible options. Capacity ~25k cells / month.

L4

Pack Assembly

Spot and laser welding, BMS integration, plastic and aluminium housings. IP65 / IP67 ratings. CAN, RS485, SMBus protocols. Capacity ~5k packs / month.

L5

Cleanroom (Medical)

Class 100K cleanroom for IEC 60601-aligned production. Gowned operators, particle counters, DHF documentation. Single-use sterile-pack assembly available.

PL

Pilot Line (NPI)

Same winding / stacking / sealing equipment as L1/L2, just at 1/10 scale. Where new geometries get qualified before MP transfer. Process parameters log directly into the MES used by L1-L3.

Quality Control

Inspection at every stage.

Every cell goes through 100% in-line OCV / IR inspection plus CCD visual check at the seal step — the routine measurements that protect against the most common cell-level failures. Other tests (capacity grading, cycle-life sampling, abuse) follow lot-based sampling per IEC 62133 / customer spec. Lot-level summary reports go out within one business day of each batch.

01

Incoming materials

Cathode, anode, separator, electrolyte, casing — QC sampled from every lot. Annual supplier audits.

  • ICP-MS purity check
  • Dimensional CMM
  • Supplier CoA verified
02

In-process

Coating thickness, alignment, slitting and stacking. SPC charts on every line, every shift.

  • Beta-gauge thickness
  • CCD edge alignment
  • Sealing peel-strength
03

Formation & grading

Multi-step formation curves, OCV/IR/capacity grading. A/B grade separation per spec.

  • 8-step formation
  • 3-point grading (V, IR, capacity)
  • Self-discharge after 7-day rest
04

Final shipment

AQL sampling on packaging, drop test on cartons, full UN 38.3 documentation per lot.

  • OCV stability after 7-day rest
  • Carton drop test
  • UN 38.3 + MSDS attached
