Privacy Policy

Effective: 1.0 · Last updated 2026

1. Who we are (data controller)

Acme Battery Co., Ltd., registered in Shenzhen, China, is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. For data protection enquiries please contact privacy@example.com or use our GDPR request page.

2. What data we collect

We minimise the personal data we collect. Specifically:

  • Inquiry data: name, work email, company, phone (optional), country, product interest, project details, and any attachments you choose to share.
  • Technical data: a hashed version of your IP address (for spam protection only), user agent, source page, referrer and UTM parameters.
  • Cookie consent: a hash of your IP+UA paired with your consent decision.

We do not collect special categories of data, financial information, or behavioural profiles.

3. Legal basis (GDPR Article 6)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): for optional analytics and marketing cookies.
  • Performance of a contract / pre-contractual steps (Art. 6(1)(b)): when you submit an inquiry, so we can respond.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): to operate the website securely, prevent spam and fraud, and maintain a basic audit trail. You may object at any time.

4. How we use your data

  • Reply to your inquiry and follow up with technical or commercial information.
  • Maintain a CRM record so we don't ask for the same information twice.
  • Comply with legal obligations including dangerous-goods documentation.
  • Improve the website (only if you consent to analytics cookies).

5. Who we share with

We use a limited number of processors:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (US/EU): Turnstile bot protection on submission forms (no personal data persisted).
  • SMTP email provider: our corporate email host transmits inquiry confirmations.
  • Hosting provider: stores the website and database.

We do not sell or share data with advertising networks, data brokers or social platforms.

6. International transfers

If you submit an inquiry from the EU/EEA, your data may be transferred to our servers in the EU and to our office in China. Such transfers are governed by appropriate safeguards including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

7. Retention

Inquiries are kept for up to 365 days from creation, then automatically marked for deletion. Deleted records are permanently removed within 30 days. Cookie consent logs are kept for 12 months. Audit logs (admin actions) are kept for 24 months.

8. Your rights (GDPR Articles 12-22)

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate data.
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please use our data subject request page. We respond within 30 days as required by Article 12.

9. Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for the full list of cookies and your control options.

10. Security

Personal data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+). Database backups are encrypted at rest. Access to admin tooling is protected by individual accounts, password hashing (bcrypt) and full audit logging. We honour the DNT (Do Not Track) header.

11. Children

This is a B2B website not intended for individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes are announced via the cookie consent banner with a new policy version, and prior consent is re-requested.
