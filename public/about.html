Our mission

To make safe, certified lithium energy storage available to every OEM — from a 30 mAh medical patch to a 100 kWh commercial cabinet — without forcing customers to compromise on form factor, certification or lead time.

What we do best

  • Custom geometries. Round, curved, stepped, ultra-thin Li-Po cells engineered around your enclosure.
  • Certification-ready manufacturing. Every shipment carries valid UN 38.3, IEC 62133 and MSDS documents.
  • OEM-grade traceability. Serial-level traceability and 100% in-line OCV/IR/CCD inspection.
  • European compliance. GDPR-compliant data handling and CE/REACH/RoHS supply.
