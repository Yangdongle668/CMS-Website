Typical Schedule

A custom project, broken into phases.

1. NDA & Brief

Mutual NDA, capture envelope, capacity, cycle, certifications.

2. Feasibility

5-day feasibility note: capacity estimate, cycle-life model, BOM cost.

3. Tooling & Sample

4-5 weeks. First-article samples + UN 38.3 test summary.

4. Qualification

2-3 weeks. Customer DV/PV testing on samples; iterate as needed.

5. Ramp

Mass production with serial-level traceability and SPC monitoring.

Engineering Capability

What we can customise.

Geometry

Round, curved (R≥25 mm), stepped, notched, asymmetric Li-Po cells.

Capacity & Voltage

30 mAh to 100+ Ah, 3.2 V LFP / 3.7 V NMC / 3.85 V high-V chemistries.

Discharge Rate

Up to 30C pulse for high-power tools and drone applications.

BMS / Connectors

PCM, fuel-gauge IC, JST/Molex/FPC connectors, custom busbars.

Pack Assembly

Spot/laser welded, plastic or aluminium housings, IP65/IP67 ratings.

Labeling & Branding

Custom shrink-wrap, logos, barcodes, lot identifiers.
