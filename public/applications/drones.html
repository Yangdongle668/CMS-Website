Drone & UAV Battery Packs

High-rate cells. Long flights. Predictable failure modes.

Where the energy goes

A drone battery has three jobs at once.

Hold enough Wh/kg for a useful mission, deliver 5–15C continuously without sagging below the BMS cutoff, and fail safely when something goes wrong at 100 m altitude. Most off-the-shelf cells nail one of those, occasionally two. A drone-grade pack has to nail all three. We ship into commercial inspection drones, agricultural sprayers, delivery quad/hex platforms, autonomous AGVs and ground robotics.

High-rate Li-Po pouch for compact platforms.

Our 5–25C continuous Li-Po cells use thicker copper foil (12–15 μm), low-resistance separator, and tab-welded multi-layer construction. Standard configurations: 3S/4S/6S in 1.5–8 Ah pouches, with a 10–15 mΩ DC-IR target at 25 °C. Burst-rate variants reach 40C for 2 seconds — enough headroom for FPV racing platforms and sudden-payload-drop maneuvers.

Every drone-grade pouch is screened on a 50-cycle burn-in at 80% DoD before tab-welding into a pack, so the soft-shorts and high-IR cells are caught before they ship.

21700 cylindrical for endurance missions.

For inspection and survey drones with 25–40 minute mission targets, a 6S2P or 6S3P 21700 pack gives 240–360 Wh in 700–1,100 g — better Wh/kg than any LiPo at the same C-rate envelope. We use INR21700-50E and -50G cells (5,000 mAh, 9.8A continuous) welded with copper-clad nickel ribbon for low pack-level IR.

For autonomous ground robots and AGVs, the same cell stacks into 14S3P (51.8V) or 13S4P modules with CAN-bus BMS, IP67 housing and forklift-style charge contacts.

Safety stack

Failure-tolerant, by design.

A drone pack that vents at altitude has to fail in a way the airframe survives. Three independent safety layers — and a paper trail every regulator will accept.

①

Cell-level screening

Every cell logged on serial number through formation, IR test at 25 °C and 80 °C, and 50-cycle burn-in. ≥ 4σ outliers rejected before tab welding.

②

Pack-level isolation

Mica or PI sheet between cells, intumescent fire-block coating on top plate, vent paths sized for the worst-case single-cell event.

③

BMS abuse cutoffs

Per-cell V/T monitoring, hardware OVP/UVP, software cutoff at 65 °C cell or 5 °C/min rise rate, brown-out hold-up to deliver telemetry to the flight controller before disconnect.

④

UN 38.3 + UL 2054

Every program ships with the test report stack required for IATA air freight, EU Battery Regulation and FAA SFAR-88 (US > 100 Wh).

⑤

CAN / SMBus telemetry

Per-cell voltage, pack current, dual-NTC temperatures, cycle count, SOH, and a 32-event fault log queryable over the same bus the flight controller already uses.

⑥

Field-replaceable design

Modular pack housings with documented connector pinouts and fuse-protected charge contacts — so end-customers can hot-swap in the field without recalibration.

Common questions

What drone OEMs ask before they commit.

What C-rate do I actually need?

Take the pack's mAh and multiply by the worst-case continuous current. Multirotor delivery drones typically pull 5–15C continuous with 25–40C burst at climb. Racing FPV pulls 60–120C in 1–2 second bursts. Spec the cell at 1.3× the continuous draw so cell temperature stays under 60 °C — this is the single biggest determinant of cycle life.

Should I pick LiPo pouch or 21700 cylindrical?

For 20–35 minute commercial multirotor missions at 5–15C, modern high-energy 21700 cells (≥ 5,000 mAh) give the best Wh/kg and Wh/$ today. Below 18 minutes or above 25C continuous, LiPo pouches still win. Above 200 Wh per pack, IATA shipping rules and EU regulatory pressure increasingly favour cylindrical formats.

How do you prevent thermal runaway propagation?

Three layers: cell-level pre-screening (reject IR + OCV outliers at 80 °C), pack-level isolation (mica spacers + intumescent coating + sized vent paths), and BMS-level cutoff (per-cell T with 65 °C limit and 5 °C/min rise-rate trip). See our thermal-runaway field guide for the test protocol.

Do you support CAN-bus or SMBus telemetry?

Standard packs ship with SMBus 1.1 fuel gauging. CAN 2.0B (with DJI A3 / Pixhawk message-map options) is available for packs 4S and above. Telemetry exposes per-cell V, pack I, two NTC temperatures, cycle count, SOH and a fault history. See PCM vs Smart Battery: when to use which.

What certifications are mandatory for international shipment?

UN 38.3 is mandatory globally. US air freight needs IATA PI965/967 plus FAA SFAR-88 above 100 Wh. EU adds CE + EU Battery Regulation (2027 enforcement). Defence and surveillance UAVs may also need MIL-PRF-32383 abuse compliance.
