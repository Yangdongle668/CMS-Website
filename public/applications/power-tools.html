Cordless Power Tool Battery Packs

High-current cells, abuse-tolerant pack design, and tabs that hold up after 30,000 trigger pulls.

Where the energy goes

A power tool pack lives a brutal life.

Trigger-on / trigger-off thousands of times. Bus-bar shocks every drop. Bag temperatures from -10 °C in winter to +55 °C in a closed truck cab. Most battery shops can't survive any single one of those modes — let alone all three repeatedly. We build packs that do, for trimmer, drill, saw, mower and lawn-tractor OEMs across North America, Europe and APAC.

21700 NMC for energy density.

Our standard pack uses INR21700-50G or 50E (5,000 mAh, 9.8A continuous) tab-welded with bus-bar copper-clad nickel for low pack-level IR. Common configurations: 5S2P (18V), 10S2P (36V/40V) and 14S2P (52V/60V). Pulse capability up to 30A per cell at 100 ms duty.

Each cell logged on serial through formation, IR test and 50-cycle burn-in before assembly. Reject IR outliers above the 4σ band — soft shorts that would otherwise show up as a runtime drop in the second year.

LFP cylindrical for cycle-life-critical platforms.

For commercial-fleet tools, lawn tractors and rental platforms, LFP cylindrical (32650 or 32700) gives 3,000-6,000 cycle life and abuse-tolerance that NMC can't match. Lower energy density (~ 165 Wh/kg) is offset by a flat discharge curve, no thermal runaway under nail-penetration, and operating range down to -20 °C with proper preheat.

We assemble both NMC and LFP variants on the same automated line — switching is a tab-weld profile change and a BMS firmware swap, not a different shop.

Build quality

What separates a good tool pack from a recall.

Most pack failures we audit fall into four categories. Each has a known fix, none of them are exotic, and none of them survive shortcut sourcing.

①

Bus-bar geometry

Copper-clad nickel ribbon welded with parallel-gap resistance settings tuned per cell vendor. Pull-tested to ≥ 4 kgf, IR-tested at 0% and 100% SOC.

②

Drop-shock survivability

Cells immobilised in a glass-fibre or PC matrix, not foam. We pass 1.5 m drop on 6 sides and 12 corners per UL 2271 §11.10 with zero internal lead displacement.

③

Connector + auth

Custom contact geometry per platform, plus 1-Wire / SMBus / proprietary handshake firmware on request — we don't bypass platform security without written authorisation from the OEM.

④

Thermal cutoff

Per-cell NTC monitoring, hardware OVP/UVP, software cutoff at 70 °C cell or 5 °C/min rise rate. Fast-charge throttle below -5 °C and above 50 °C.

⑤

UL 2271 + UN 38.3 file

Every program ships with full UL 2271 abuse pass, UN 38.3 transport report, and the EU Battery Regulation digital battery passport (2027-ready).

⑥

Field-replaceable

Modular pack housings with fuse-protected charge contacts and a documented connector pinout. End-customers can hot-swap without recalibration.

Common questions

What tool OEMs ask before they commit.

How much current does my tool actually pull?

Drills and impact drivers pull 8–15A continuous, 25–40A at trigger-pull peak. Circular saws and angle grinders pull 20–35A continuous through cuts. Spec the cell at 1.4× the worst continuous draw to keep pack temperature under 55 °C — that single decision determines whether your pack delivers 800 or 1,500 cycles.

NMC or LFP for a 40V cordless platform?

NMC (≈ 250 Wh/kg) wins on runtime per gram for handheld tools where weight matters. LFP (≈ 165 Wh/kg) wins on cycle life (3,000+ vs 1,500), thermal abuse safety, and cold-weather operation — best for rental fleets, lawn tractors and equipment running 8-hour commercial shifts.

How do you weld 21700 tabs without damaging the cell?

Parallel-gap resistance welding for the nickel-plated steel positive cap, ultrasonic-assisted for the aluminum negative. Welder energy is calibrated per cell vendor (Samsung, LG, BAK, EVE) to within ±5% because can-wall thickness varies 0.18–0.22 mm. Every batch starts with a destructive pull-test on five samples to confirm joint strength.

Which certifications are required?

UL 2271 is the de-facto North American standard for cordless tool batteries above 18V. UN 38.3 is mandatory for any lithium battery in commerce. CE + EU Battery Regulation apply in Europe (digital battery passport from 2027). Costco / Home Depot / Amazon programs often add their own qualification stack on top.

Can you build a replacement for an existing tool platform?

Yes — we reverse-engineer connector pinout, communication protocol and mechanical interface from a sample tool. Auth-locked platforms (DeWalt FlexVolt, Milwaukee M18 RedLink+) require written authorisation from the OEM; we do not bypass platform security without it.
