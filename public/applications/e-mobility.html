Where we ship

From e-bikes to last-mile fleets.

We design and assemble 36V to 96V battery packs with integrated BMS, CAN-bus telemetry, and IP67 housings for e-mobility OEMs across Europe, North America and Southeast Asia. Common platforms: city e-bikes, cargo e-bikes, kick-scooters, seated mopeds, food-delivery scooters, AGVs, mobility scooters and small last-mile delivery vehicles.

21700 NMC for energy-dense LEV platforms.

Standard configurations: 10S5P (36V, 25 Ah / 900 Wh), 13S5P (48V, 25 Ah / 1,200 Wh), 14S6P (52V, 30 Ah / 1,560 Wh) and 20S6P (72V, 30 Ah / 2,160 Wh). All built around INR21700-50G cells (5,000 mAh, 9.8A continuous), tab-welded with copper-clad nickel ribbon. Pack DC-IR target ≤ 12 mΩ at 25 °C — the resistance budget that keeps the BMS from tripping on a cold-morning hill climb.

LFP for fleet platforms.

For shared scooter fleets, AGVs, and food-delivery platforms where 8-year cycle life beats peak energy density, we ship 32700 LFP packs. 14S5P (44.8V) gives ~ 25 Ah at 4,000+ cycle life and a thermal-runaway profile that no NMC chemistry matches under nail-penetration. Our LFP line is the same ISO 9001 cell, just a different chemistry — switching is a BMS firmware load and a tab-weld profile change, not a different supply chain.

What goes inside

Pack design that survives the real world.

A LEV battery lives outside, gets dropped, charged in a hallway, and needs to last 4–6 years before warranty claims start. Six engineering decisions separate a fleet pack from a recall.

①

Low-IR pack architecture

Bus-bar copper-clad nickel ribbon, balanced parallel paths, pack DC-IR target ≤ 12 mΩ at 25 °C. Keeps voltage sag under 6% at full continuous draw.

②

IP67 housing + drainage

Aluminium or ABS+PC enclosure with rubber-gasket seal, internal drain channels, vent valve rated IP69K. Passes 1m / 30min immersion + 100 thermal cycles -20°C ↔ 60°C.

③

Smart CAN-bus BMS

Per-cell V/T, pack I, cycle count, SOH and 32-event fault log over CAN 2.0B. Bluetooth + GSM telemetry options for fleet platforms.

④

Charge-port intelligence

Standard XLR / GX16 / proprietary. Optional handshake auth with charger to prevent wrong-charger fires. Fast-charge throttle below 0 °C.

⑤

Cell-isolation matrix

PI sheet between cells, intumescent fire-block coating, vent paths sized for single-cell event. NCA/NMC packs get an extra mica top plate.

⑥

UL 2271 + EN 15194 + UN 38.3

Every program ships with the regulatory file stack required for North American CPSC compliance, EU EN 15194 e-bike type approval and IATA shipping.

Common questions

What LEV OEMs ask before they commit.

What voltage and configuration for an e-bike?

36V (10S) is the EU 250W standard, 48V (13S) is the global default and 52V (14S) is gaining ground for cargo bikes and speed pedelecs. E-scooters span 36V (entry) to 72V (motorbike-class). Above 96V you cross regulatory thresholds — the platform becomes a registered LEV.

18650 or 21700 — which cell format?

21700 wins on Wh/kg, Wh/$ and pack-level integration today. A 13S5P 21700 delivers ~ 25 Ah / 1,200 Wh in less weight than 13S6P 18650. Choose 18650 only when the existing battery housing fixes the cell footprint.

Do I need UL 2849 for the US market?

Yes — the CPSC rule effective 2024 requires UL 2849 (system-level) for any e-bike sold in the US. NYC, California and several insurers add their own riders. EN 15194 + EN 50604-1 + UN 38.3 covers the same ground for the EU.

How do you stop the BMS cutting out on hill climbs?

Three things: (1) keep pack DC-IR ≤ 12 mΩ to limit sag under 6%, (2) set the BMS overcurrent threshold 1.3× above worst-case continuous with 200 ms blanking, (3) cut at 2.8V/cell rather than 2.5V to protect chemistry over the life of the pack.

Can one pack platform support NMC and LFP?

Yes. The mechanical housing is the same; the differences are tab-weld profile and BMS firmware (LFP charges to 3.65V/cell vs NMC 4.20V, and needs a different fuel-gauge model). We routinely ship both variants on the 13S and 14S e-bike platforms.
