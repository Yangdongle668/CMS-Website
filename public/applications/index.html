Why we group by application

Each industry brings its own physics — and its own paperwork.

A medical battery and a drone battery look similar on a scope, but the abuse profile, certification stack, supply-chain rigour and lifecycle expectations are completely different. We pre-tune our chemistry, BMS topology and QA documentation per application so projects move faster.
