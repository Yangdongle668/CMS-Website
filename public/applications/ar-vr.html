AR / VR Glasses

Every gram on a headset matters. Every millimetre in a temple matters more.

The Challenge

Slim temples, all-day runtime.

AR glasses push battery engineering to the edge: the cell has to fit in a < 6 mm temple, survive 500+ charge cycles, power displays and radios, and never get hot against skin. We've shipped cells into glasses and headsets from 8 mAh (sensor-only smart glasses) to 2,500 mAh (standalone VR).

Ultra-thin pouch cells

Down to 0.45 mm thickness, with curved and stepped profiles that hug the inside of a temple. Our HV LCO chemistry at 4.48 V gives +8% volumetric energy density versus standard 4.40 V cells.

Every cell is screened for volumetric swelling under 50-cycle and 85 °C / 85% RH aging — critical for hermetically-sealed eyewear enclosures.

Thermal-aware BMS

Integrated fuel gauging with skin-contact temperature limiting. The BMS de-rates charging when the user is wearing the device, protecting comfort and cell chemistry simultaneously.

Supports wireless-charging front ends, USB-PD, and custom spring-contact pogo interfaces used in most AR docking cradles.
