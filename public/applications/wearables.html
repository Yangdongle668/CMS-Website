Wearables

Round, curved, stacked, tiny. Wearables ask for cells that shouldn't geometrically exist — we build them anyway.

Form Factor First

The cell bends around the product, not the other way.

Wearables live or die on industrial design. We start from your enclosure — ring, band, earbud, patch — and engineer the cell to fit it, with matching BMS and terminals pre-integrated.

Curved & shaped pouches

Single or double curvature, arbitrary polygon outlines, stepped cells that hug two enclosure heights in one piece. Minimum bend radius: 25 mm for 3 mm cells.

Thickness tolerances held to ±0.05 mm for tight ID enclosures.

Round coin cells for earbuds & rings

Rechargeable Li-ion button cells from 4 mm to 16 mm diameter, 1.0 mm to 5.8 mm thickness. Pair with our charging-case cell for a complete TWS solution — we ship both sides.
