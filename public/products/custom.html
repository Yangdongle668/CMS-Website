One of a hundred tiny compromises on a wearable or AR headset is usually the battery. Let's change that.

Capabilities

Anything the cell can physically be.

Every parameter — chemistry, capacity, shape, thickness, terminals, BMS — is adjustable. Tell us the enclosure and the duty cycle, and we'll propose the smallest, lightest, longest-lasting option that still passes certification.

Shape & Geometry

Rectangular, curved, stepped, L-shape, round — pouch cells down to 0.45 mm thickness, cylindrical cells in non-standard diameters.

⚡

Chemistry Selection

LCO, NMC, LiFePO4, LTO and high-voltage variants. We match chemistry to your energy/power/cycle-life trade-off.

⚙

Integrated BMS

Protection PCM, fuel gauge, smart-battery (SMBus/I²C), wireless-charging front-end — tuned to your host firmware.

⌁

Terminals & Harness

Custom tabs, spring contacts, pogo pins, ZIF connectors, overmolded wire harnesses with strain relief.

✦

High-Voltage Cells

4.45 V and 4.48 V platforms for devices needing +8% energy density at the same volume.

✓

Certification Plan

IEC 62133, UN 38.3, UL 2054, KC, PSE — we handle testing in-house or with accredited partners.
