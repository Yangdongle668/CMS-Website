Common Questions

Answers from the engineering team.

What is the typical MOQ?

Custom designs typically start at 3,000 pcs. Catalogue cells start at 500 pcs. For NPI samples we accept 50-200 pcs.

How long from RFQ to first sample?

Catalogue items: 3-5 days. Custom designs: 25-35 days from drawing freeze to first samples (includes tooling).

Are your batteries certified for air shipping?

Yes. Every shipment includes a valid UN 38.3 test summary. We air-ship from Hong Kong / Shenzhen with proper IATA labelling and packing.

Do you sign NDAs?

Routinely. We sign mutual NDAs before any drawings or BOM exchange.

Can you support medical-device programs?

Yes — we operate an ISO 13485 documented line with biocompatibility-aligned materials, leakage testing per IEC 60601, and full DHF support.
