Your Rights

Submit a GDPR data request.

Under EU GDPR Articles 15-22, you can ask us to access, rectify or delete the personal data we hold about you. We will verify your identity by email and respond within 30 days.

After submission, we will email a verification link to the address above. Click it to confirm the request. We will respond within 30 days as required by GDPR Article 12. You may also email privacy@example.com directly.
