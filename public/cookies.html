What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. We use a mix of cookies and localStorage entries to operate the site and remember your preferences.

Analytics (opt-in)

If you opt in, we may set first-party analytics cookies to count visits and understand which pages are useful. These are aggregated; we do not use them to identify individual visitors. Today we do not deploy any third-party analytics by default.

Marketing (opt-in)

If you opt in, we may set marketing cookies to measure the effectiveness of trade-press advertising. Today no marketing cookies are deployed by default.

Third parties

Cloudflare Turnstile may temporarily set short-lived cookies on form pages to detect automated bots. Turnstile is privacy-preserving and does not track users across sites.
